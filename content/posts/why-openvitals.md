15 years ago I installed my first distro of ubuntu, making me fall in love with linux and this idea of open source and community driven projects, slowly but surely I started my deep dive in android modding communities and built the grounds for what was going to be my professional career. 

I always saw technology as a driver of human improvement, something that in kind hands can bring so much good to the world, and even we see that it can also be used for terrible purposes as of this day I have not stopped believing in it.

When I started living in Estonia I got in contact to what I thought it was a digital society utopia, with the palm of your hand you could access all your information and sign any kind of documents, even in your cabin in the middle of the woods.

Even if after 5 years of living there I have a more measured view of Estonia as a whole, I can still say that what they have achieved is unique. Especially regarding health, I was able to access every data of it without the need to store papers, call doctors, track down studies, request consultations. Everything was always at my disposition letting me take the best decisions I could.
